# Topical Authority — pools.codev.id

## Role and boundary

`pools.codev.id` currently presents a pool-design and construction service template. Its knowledge plan should help residential and commercial owners move from need definition through design, construction, handover, operation, maintenance, repair, and renovation. Transactional routes own quotation, availability, scheduling, and contracting. Exact engineering, structural, electrical, hydraulic, chemical, safety, accessibility, permit, and cost claims require project-specific evidence and qualified review.

## Context used

- Homepage signals: “Crafting Dream Pools,” custom pool design/construction, tailored designs, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.
- Repository: 136 tracked files.
- Sitemap: 13 entries covering homepage, about, services, contact, blog/sample posts, author/category/feed, and error routes.
- No meaningful product/service taxonomy is implemented; the topical plan therefore uses the confirmed pool-builder entity and lifecycle rather than placeholder service labels.

## Ignored template noise

Placeholder tokens such as `[SERVICE1:TITLE]`, generic sample posts, author/category/feed routes, error pages, repeated WordPress metadata, theme assets, and USA-specific template language were excluded from topic decisions. Indonesia-specific claims remain evidence gates until the deployment role and service geography are confirmed.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub. Need/type pages link to design and site survey; design pages link to structure, finishes, and systems; construction pages link to acceptance; operating symptoms link to safety, maintenance, repair, and renovation decisions. Commercial routes receive links only after project requirements and evidence needs are clear.
